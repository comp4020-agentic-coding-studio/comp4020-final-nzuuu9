# Crit 8 reflection

I missed the scheduled session and completed this milestone afterwards, on my own schedule.

**Breakthrough.** Claude's strongest case for building a dinner-coordination app was that two housemates might accidentally buy the same ingredient. It asked me for an evening when that had actually happened, and I couldn't recall one — the justification didn't survive contact with my own memory. What I could recall, clearly, was friends saying "anything is fine" and going along with the first suggestion rather than naming what they actually wanted. That recollection, not the duplicate-purchase scenario, is what I asked Claude to build towards: a private, explicit mark for every option, recorded in [658808a](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-nzuuu9/commit/658808ad81ff2cd0bfbd4bae818145fc4362215e).

**What it changed.** I want to be the kind of developer who checks a justification against a specific memory before building on it, not just a plausible-sounding one. A scenario an agent proposes can sound reasonable and still be fiction; the discipline is noticing when I can't actually recall it happening, and redirecting to something I can.
